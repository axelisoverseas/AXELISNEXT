# Claude Browser: language partner enquiry forms and rate discovery

Paste the block below into a Claude session with browser access.

**Why this matters commercially.** The Ausbildung and Chancenkarte premium bundle
is priced at ₹1,95,000 against a delivered cost of ₹1,28,000. The largest single
line in that cost is ₹62,000 for German A1 to B2 delivery, and that figure is
inherited from an internal PDF that nobody has verified. At ₹62,000 it implies
₹103 per contact hour over roughly 600 hours, which only holds in large cohorts.
If the real rate is higher, the bundle loses money on every candidate.

This task exists to replace that assumption with a real, sourced number.

Three emails have already been drafted and are sitting in the Axelis Gmail for
Goethe-Institut India, Lingoda and telc. This browser task covers the enquiry
forms and the public rate discovery those emails cannot get at.

---

> study-abroad company launching Ausbildung and Chancenkarte programmes for
> candidates moving to Germany. Both need certified German from A1 to B1 or B2.
>
> The goal is a defensible cost per learner for a full A1 to B2 pathway,
> delivered to cohorts of Indian learners, plus the partnership route for each
> provider.
>

> - **Do not accept terms, sign up for an account, or agree to a contract.**
> - You may complete a **partnership or B2B enquiry form** using the company
>   details in the block below. Before you submit any form, show the user what
>   you are about to send and wait for them to say go.
> - If a form asks for learner volumes, use "cohorts of 15 to 30, multiple
>   intakes per year" and nothing larger. Do not invent bigger numbers.
> - Decline cookie banners to the most private option available.
> - Report as you go, do not batch to the end.
>
> ### Company details to use on forms
>
> - Company: Axelis Overseas Education Pvt Ltd
> - Contact: Naveen Bammidi
> - Email: naveen@overseeducation.com
> - Phone: +91 9098522711

>   certification for vocational migration candidates

> ### Task 1: Lingoda B2B

>
> ### Task 2: telc

> ### Task 3: Goethe-Institut India

> Open `goethe.de/ins/in/en/spr/kur.html` and the Max Mueller Bhavan pages for

>
> Then state plainly: **what does a full A1 to B2 pathway cost per learner at
> the cheapest credible option, and at the most expensive?** That range is the

## What to do with the answer

If the verified cost per learner for A1 to B2 lands:

- **at or under ₹80,000**, the ₹1,95,000 premium holds with a healthy margin.
- **between ₹80,000 and ₹1,10,000**, the premium needs to move to about
  ₹2,25,000, and the partner channel needs re-checking because partner margin
  falls as the premium rises.
- **above ₹1,10,000**, A1 to B2 cannot be bundled at this price at all. The
  premium has to be scoped to A1 to A2 with an explicit session cap, and B1 and
  B2 sold separately.

Do not write the Ausbildung or Chancenkarte charter fee tables until this
number is known.
