## Three things to settle before you send anything

**1. 48 hours for onboarding is not available, and asking for it will cost you standing.**

Nimisha's own email of 18 September says it plainly:

> "any onboarding and activation process typically takes approximately **15 days** to go live, subject to successful completion of all required formalities, approvals and dealer consent."

That is her own stated process, not an excuse invented for you. Demanding 48-hour activation asks her to break a published SLA, which she cannot do and will not escalate on your behalf to try.

**What *is* achievable in 48 hours is a written decision on category eligibility.** That is the actual blocker. Once it is a yes, the 15-day clock starts and it is administrative. Aim the pressure there.

**2. The RBI grievance route is the wrong tool, and using it now would be a mistake.**

Bajaj's escalation matrix — Grievance Redressal Officer, then Principal Nodal Officer, then RBI Ombudsman — exists for **customer service failures**: unresolved complaints, mis-selling, unfair practice toward a borrower.

You are a **prospective merchant whose category application is pending**. That is a commercial negotiation, not a grievance. Filing there would be misdirected, would be closed without action, and Nimisha would hear about it while she is still the person deciding your case.

Keep it in reserve for an actual service failure — for instance, if the submitted KYC pack is neither actioned nor returned for a further 30 days.

**3. "Legally we are good to go" is true about the business, and not about this.**

Running unaccredited private training in India is lawful, and nothing here is blocked by regulation. But no law compels a private NBFC to finance any particular merchant. Bajaj may decline a category on its own risk appetite, and framing the ask as an entitlement invites a firm no in writing, which is worse than the present soft position.

The leverage you actually hold is commercial: a live pipeline, three transactions waiting, and a product set that now matches the category they already finance.
