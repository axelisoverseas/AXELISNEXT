# Certification playbook — what to apply for, in what order

Compiled 21 September 2026. Every URL verified live on that date.
Companion to `EDUVANZ_TRACK.md` and `../CLAIMS_SUBSTANTIATION.md`.

**Purpose:** get Axelis to a credential a lender's credit officer can verify
independently, fastest. Ordered by *time to obtain*, with the honest note on
what each one is actually worth.

---

## Read this before applying for anything

**No lender requires accreditation.** Bajaj publishes no accreditation
prerequisite. Neither does Eduvanz. Empanelment is driven by commercial and
credit underwriting: entity, vintage, financials, banking turnover, refund
exposure, category risk.

So a credential is a **persuasion asset for the category-fit argument** — "we
sell a defined, assessed course, not an open-ended service" — not a gate you
are currently failing. Do not spend three months waiting for one before
talking to lenders. Run both in parallel.

**The thing that actually moves the needle is structural**, and it is already
done: fixed duration, published curriculum, contact hours, assessment against
a rubric, a verifiable certificate, and a published refund policy that covers
every programme. That is `/programmes`.

## Tier 1 — days, do these first

### 1. Udyam / MSME registration

- **URL:** https://udyamregistration.gov.in
- **Time:** same day, online, Aadhaar + PAN
- **Cost:** free
- **Worth:** listed as an acceptable business proof in Bajaj's own document
  list ("Business Proof 1 – GSTIN/MSME"). If Axelis does not already hold it,
  this is a free item on a lender checklist.
- **Check first:** Axelis already holds DPIIT Startup India recognition. Confirm
  whether Udyam is already on file before re-registering.

### 2. Confirm what is already held

Before applying for anything new, get these into one folder:

- DPIIT Startup India recognition certificate
- British Council certification
- AIRC certification
- GST certificate (22AAZCA0637P1Z5)
- Certificate of Incorporation (CIN U85500CT2023PTC014913)

**Important:** these accredit *the company*, not *the credential*. They belong
in a lender file. They must never appear on a certificate, because that would
imply the certificate is accredited by them. The specimen certificate already
says so explicitly and that line must stay.

## Tier 2 — weeks, highest ratio of effort to result

### 3. ISO 21001:2018 — Educational Organisations Management System

- **What it certifies:** how the organisation runs teaching, assessment and
  learner support. **Not** the quality or recognition of any course.
- **Time:** weeks, depending on the certification body
- **Cost:** NOT VERIFIED. Every source found was a marketing page. Get three
  written quotes.
- **The trap:** ISO 21001 accreditation has only existed since spring 2022 and
  the market is full of mills. One search result advertised it "starting at
  ₹4,000" — that is a worthless piece of paper.
- **The rule:** only use a certification body listed on the **NABCB directory**,
  and insist the certificate carries the **NABCB or IAF mark**.
  - Directory: https://nabcb.qci.org.in
- **Worth to a lender:** low to moderate. Supporting evidence, not a centrepiece.
  It does speak directly to the delivery and refund questions a credit team asks.

### 4. ISO 9001:2015 — Quality Management System

Generic QMS, same caveats, usually quoted alongside 21001 by the same body.
Ask for both in the same quote.

## Tier 3 — months, but this is the real one

### 5. MEP/Q4401 — Career and Education Counsellor, NSQF Level 5

**This is the exact-fit national qualification and nothing else comes close.**

- **Owner:** Management & Entrepreneurship and Professional Skills Council
  (MEPSC), a Sector Skill Council under MSDE
- **NQR code:** `2022/ETR/MEPSC/06152` v1.0 · **NSQF Level 5** · 18 credits
- **Approved:** 21st NSQC, 28 July 2022
- **Qualification file:** https://nqr.gov.in/qualification/file/QF_Career%20and%20Education%20Counsellor.pdf
- **Official description, verbatim:** a Career and Education Counsellor
  *"keep[s] abreast with developments in the education and labour market to
  link candidates to career and education opportunities **in India and
  abroad**."*
- **NCO alignment:** `NCO-2015/2423.0100`, which names **"Admission Counsellor"**
  and **"Education Consultant"** in the national framework
- **Delivery:** 290 hours classroom, or 250 blended. *The "540 hours" in the
  file is the sum of both mode rows, not one learner's programme length.*
- **Why it matters:** a credit officer can verify it themselves at nqr.gov.in.
  Nothing else on this list is independently checkable that way.

#### ⚠️ The trap, do not get this backwards

**MEP/Q4401 certifies the COUNSELLOR, not the student.** Entry requires a
graduate degree; progression is to "Education Consultant".

It gives Axelis a **brand new financeable programme** — *"Become a certified
Career and Education Counsellor, NSQF Level 5, MEPSC-aligned, 290 hours,
assessed"* — sold to graduates and career-changers.

It does **not** reclassify the fee a student going abroad pays. That problem
is solved structurally by `/programmes`, not by this qualification.

#### The route

1. NSDC training-partner registration
2. **Centre accreditation** — includes a **physical inspection of a physical
   training centre**. This is the real constraint for a company without a
   classroom, and it is what decides whether this takes weeks or months.
3. Job-role affiliation, **~₹6,000 per job role**, auto-granted once accredited
   and paid

**Fees and timelines beyond that ₹6,000 are NOT VERIFIED.** Every "15-day NSDC
registration" claim online came from resellers. Discount them entirely.

#### The two calls that decide everything

Make these before committing to anything:

- **NSDC Centre Accreditation & Affiliation:** 1800-123-9626, Mon–Sat 9am–6pm
- **MEPSC:** ceo@mepsc.in, 011-24645100 (SPOC: Col. Anil Kumar Pokhriyal)

Ask exactly two things:

1. The current TP registration + CAAF + accreditation + affiliation fee card,
   and which portal is live — SMART or Skill India Digital Hub.
2. **Whether a Bengaluru Pvt Ltd can affiliate MEP/Q4401 in BLENDED mode, and
   the minimum physical infrastructure for centre accreditation.**

**The blended-mode answer is the whole question.** If blended is permitted with
minimal premises, this is weeks. If a full physical centre is required, it is
months and possibly not worth it yet.

## Tier 4 — parallel, language-specific

### 6. Goethe-Institut / Alliance Française preparation alignment

The CEFR B1 language programme already maps to a published international
framework and prepares for a third-party examination. Formal recognition as a
preparation provider would make the strongest programme stronger.

- Goethe-Institut India: https://www.goethe.de/ins/in/en/index.html
- Alliance Française: https://www.afindia.org

Status: to be scoped. No published partner route found; needs a direct enquiry.

## Explicitly ruled out, with reasons

| Route | Why not |
|---|---|
| **NASSCOM FutureSkills Prime** | MeitY digital and deep-tech only — AI, cloud, cyber, data. NASSCOM eligibility keys off revenue from IT services. **This is why AlmaBetter's route cannot be copied.** |
| **NCVET Awarding Body recognition** | Real, but a regulatory recognition with governance, financial and QA scrutiny. Revisit in year 2–3, not now. |
| **NABET (QCI)** | Known schemes are school accreditation and EIA-consultant accreditation. Scheme fit for a commercial short-course provider is unconfirmed. Needs a call before any effort. |
| **Any "NSDC registration in 7 days" reseller** | registerkaro.in, udyogsuvidhakendra.in, legalbabu.com, nsdccourses.com and similar. Their claimed timelines and criteria do not match the official guidelines. |

## The order to actually work in

1. **Today, free:** confirm Udyam/MSME, and collect the five existing
   certificates into one folder.
2. **Today, free:** the two phone calls above. The blended-mode answer decides
   the NSDC question.
3. **This week:** three ISO quotes from NABCB-listed bodies only.
4. **In parallel, not after:** Eduvanz, Auxilo and Avanse outreach. No lender
   requires a credential, so waiting for one costs you months for nothing.
5. **Months:** NSDC → centre accreditation → MEP/Q4401 affiliation, if the
   blended answer makes it viable.
