## 2. Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

Three tight ICPs. Speak only to one per reel / ad — broadcasting to all three at once dilutes the hook.

### ICP A — UG aspirant (16–19 years old)
- **Pain**: Parents pushing them to take SAT/IELTS, doesn't know which, school has terrible counsellors, batch classes feel like school
- **What they Google**: *"is SAT or IELTS easier"*, *"how to crack PTE in 1 month"*, *"IELTS coaching near me"*
- **Channel**: Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts
- **Tone**: friendly, specific, anti-fluff
- **Hook**: *"If you're 17 and confused about IELTS vs TOEFL vs PTE, here's the 30-second answer."*

### ICP B — PG aspirant (21–25 years old)
- **Pain**: Working a 9-to-5, can't attend 6 PM batches, needs flexibility, has been quoted ₹40,000 elsewhere for the same syllabus
- **What they Google**: *"IELTS evening classes online"*, *"PTE in 2 weeks"*, *"TOEFL vs IELTS for USA"*
- **Channel**: LinkedIn (sponsored posts), Google Search Ads, YouTube Shorts
- **Tone**: confident, ROI-focused
- **Hook**: *"₹6,000. Twelve 1-on-1 IELTS sessions. Schedule around your job. Miss your band? We keep teaching free."*

### ICP C — Parent / family decision-maker (40–55 years old)
- **Pain**: Doesn't want to spend ₹40K on coaching, wants accountability, wants to see proof
- **What they Google**: *"best IELTS coaching Bangalore"*, *"is Axelis Overseas legit"*, *"refund policy IELTS coaching"*
- **Channel**: WhatsApp forwards, Facebook ads, Google Search Ads
- **Tone**: trust-first, evidence-led
- **Hook**: *Lead with DPIIT / British Council / AIRC certifications, the visa-received-Fall-2025 cohort, and the score guarantee.*

## 3. Channel strategy

### Instagram Reels — primary channel
- **Cadence**: 4–5 reels/week minimum for the first 60 days
- **Length**: 15–45 seconds (anything over 45s drops engagement sharply on IG)
- **First 3 seconds**: face on camera + price hook OR comparison hook. No logo intro.
- **CTA**: ALWAYS link in bio → `/test-prep` (use the storyboard CTA card with `Enrol via Razorpay`)
- **Trending audio**: Use trending sounds but lip-sync the hook on your own audio — IG penalises pure-trending-audio educational content less when there's spoken value

### YouTube Shorts — secondary, SEO upside
- Same content as Reels, repost identical
- Title format: `IELTS 7.5 in 6 weeks — exactly what we tested with 1,200 students` (specific + curiosity)
- Description format: 2 lines + link to `/test-prep` + 3 hashtags

### LinkedIn — for the 23–30 working pro segment
- Post 2x/week
- Format: text post + 1 student photo (with consent), end with link
- Best-performing hook: *"₹40,000 IELTS coaching is a scam. Here's what should cost less than ₹10K instead."* (controversy + concrete number)

### WhatsApp — highest conversion channel
- Status updates: 1 per day with a single placement photo + pricing
- Broadcast list for current/past students' parents: send the page once, ask for referrals

### Google Ads — paid acquisition
- **Primary keyword set**: `IELTS coaching Bangalore`, `PTE coaching online`, `1 on 1 IELTS classes`, `SAT coaching India`, `DELF coaching online`, `Goethe Institut coaching Bangalore`
- **Ad copy template**: `1-on-1 {EXAM} Coaching — ₹{PRICE}. 8+ year tutors. Miss your band, we keep teaching free. Free demo.`
- **Landing page**: `/test-prep?utm_source=google&utm_campaign={exam}` (UTMs survive into Razorpay metadata if you wire them — useful for tracking which channel converts)

## 12. What NOT to do

- ❌ Don't run pure-discount reels ("50% off!"). Premium positioning dies the moment you discount.
- ❌ Don't bury the price. Show it in the first frame of every reel where it's the hook.
- ❌ Don't mention "we are the best" or "no.1 in India". Show, don't claim. Use the receipts, the placements, the visa stamps.
- ❌ Don't broadcast the same caption + hashtags forever. IG kills reach when it senses copy-paste.
- ❌ Don't promote ALL 7 tracks in one reel. One reel = one test = one ICP.
- ❌ Don't gate the demo behind a form before showing the price. Form-first kills mobile conversion.

---

End of playbook. Ship reels weekly, measure with the table above, iterate.
