# Ambitio reference, read 29 Sep 2026

Full-page capture of ambitio.club, taken locally with Playwright. What we borrow, and what we don't.

## Borrow
- **Hero:** full-bleed, warm, cinematic image; centred headline where the key phrase is set in an
  *italic serif* in the accent colour; one line under it; one pill CTA. A small pill above the
  headline carries the proof ("20,339+ students guided") with an avatar stack.
- **Section rhythm:** small grey pill label → headline mixing sans with an italic-serif phrase →
  one-line subline. Centred. Generous whitespace on an off-white (#fafafa) ground.
- **Proof cards:** masonry testimonial cards: face, name, programme, a short quote, and the
  university as a chip at the foot.
- **Buttons:** dark pill buttons with a soft gradient; a secondary outlined pill.
- **Close:** "Ready to elevate your *study abroad journey?*" + one dark pill. Huge faint wordmark
  in the footer.

## Don't borrow
- Their red/crimson palette and wordmark: we keep Axelis navy #16265C and blue #1D4ED8, Lato.
- "Built for the top 1%" positioning: we serve students across India, marks included.
- Claims we cannot back (e.g. "7x better outcomes"). Every figure on our pages is one we publish.

## Axelis version
- Italic serif accent: Instrument Serif italic, in --color-axelis.
- Hero image = a thumbnail-grade composite (real student cutout, bold 1–4 word text, flag),
  rendered with the axelis-thumbnails skill; consent gate applies.
- One page = one YouTube episode. The video is the page.
