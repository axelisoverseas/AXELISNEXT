1. What we undertake

A named counsellor for the duration. A published syllabus with stated contact hours. Assessment against published criteria rather than attendance alone. A certificate carrying a unique ID, verifiable at overseeducation.com/verify.

2. What we do not promise

No one can guarantee a visa, an admission, an employer contract or an exam score, and we do not. Those decisions belong to universities, missions, examiners and employers. We undertake the work: the preparation, the submission and the deadlines.

3. Refund commitment

Concierge-tier programmes name a specific outcome in writing and a specific refund percentage if it is not delivered. That percentage ranges from 25% to 40% depending on the programme and is stated on the programme page. It is issued regardless of how far into the programme you are.

Programmes outside the concierge tier carry no outcome-linked refund. They remain covered by the tiered cancellation refunds in the published policy.

4. Cancellation

7 business days from payment: full fee less ₹2,500. Day 8 to 15: 75%. Day 16 to 30: 50%. Day 31 to 45: 25%. Day 46 onward, or on completion of 75% of the programme: nil. EMI-financed refunds are routed to the financing partner.

5. Your undertakings

Accurate information and genuine documents. Missed deadlines or withheld material may make an outcome unachievable, and where that is the cause, clause 3 does not apply.

This draft has not been reviewed by counsel. It is an internal template. Do not issue it to a student until it has been checked, particularly clauses 3 and 5.
