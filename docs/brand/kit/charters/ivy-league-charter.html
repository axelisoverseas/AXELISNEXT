Student charter

Ivy League Charter

Version 1.0 · Effective 29 September 2026 · New charter

For Master's and MBA applications to the eight Ivy League universities, with top US universities on the same list. This is the agreement between Axelis Overseas Education Pvt Ltd ("Axelis") and you, the student.

1. What we do for you

  • Profile evaluation, and a shortlist of programmes at the eight Ivy League universities (Brown, Columbia, Cornell, Dartmouth, Harvard, the University of Pennsylvania, Princeton and Yale), balanced with top US universities. The list becomes your locked preference list on the status tracker.
  • Essay and SOP strategy with feedback on every draft, a US-format résumé, guidance on letters of recommendation, and mock interviews for programmes that interview.
  • Application submission; scholarship, fellowship and assistantship applications where offered; education loans through 25+ lending partners.
  • I-20, SEVIS and F-1 visa filing with a mock visa interview, housing assistance and pre-departure support.

3. When your money comes back

No offer from your preference list. The ₹19,999 service fee is refunded in full if no university on your preference list, Ivy League or not, makes you an offer.

Your preference list must contain at least five universities, at least two of them Ivy League.

Visa refused, and not down to you. The success fee you have paid is refunded in full if your F-1 visa is refused for a reason not attributable to you.

Approved refunds are paid to the original payment method within 14 days.

4. When it does not

  • You receive an offer from any university on your preference list, Ivy League or not.
  • You leave the process yourself after the first shortlist has been shared.
  • You decline an offer from a university on your list. The service fee is not refunded; a success fee is due only if you accept an Ivy League offer.

No admission, scholarship, fellowship or visa is guaranteed. Axelis is not affiliated with, endorsed by or acting for the Ivy League or any of its universities; their names describe where we apply on your behalf.

5. Your part

  1. Preference list. You rank the universities you want on the status tracker. Once you confirm, the order is locked. The same list appears in your student portal.
  2. Only the list counts. Refunds are assessed against the universities on your preference list and nothing else.
  3. Genuine information. Every document and detail you give us must be true. Forged documents, fraud or defamation end this agreement and may attract liability under the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023, including Sections 316, 318 and 336 to 338.
