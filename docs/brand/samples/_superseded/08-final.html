§4 · The written outcome guarantee

What happens if we fail is written down before you pay

Axelis does not guarantee admission — nobody honestly can. It guarantees a defined remedy on a defined failure, and publishes both.

§4.1A shortlist of at least six universities, agreed in writing before applications open.
§4.2No offer from any shortlisted university — 60% of the fee returned within 30 days.
§4.3Not covered: visa refusal on grounds of document fraud, or withdrawal by the student.

Questions we get asked

The awkward ones, answered

Do you guarantee admission?

No. Nobody can. We guarantee a remedy — clause 4.2 — if you receive no offer from any university on the shortlist we agreed in writing. That is a different and enforceable promise.

Why only three programmes?

Because each one is capped by intake and staffed by a named lead. A cheaper self-serve tier would mean guaranteeing an outcome we cannot control, which is exactly what the rest of the category does.

What is not covered?

Visa refusal on grounds of document fraud or misrepresentation, withdrawal by the student, and failure to sit a required test on the agreed date. All three are in §4.3, in full, before you pay.

Are you paid by universities?

The programme fee is paid by you. That is what lets the shortlist be built around your outcome rather than around a commission table.

Read clause 4 before you speak to anyone else.
