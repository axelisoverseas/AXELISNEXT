§4 · The written outcome guarantee

What happens if we fail is written down before you pay

Axelis does not guarantee admission — no one honestly can. It guarantees a defined remedy on a defined failure, and publishes both.

§4.1A shortlist of at least six universities, agreed in writing before applications open.
§4.2No offer from any shortlisted university — 60% of the fee returned within 30 days.
§4.3Not covered: visa refusal on grounds of document fraud, or withdrawal by the student.
