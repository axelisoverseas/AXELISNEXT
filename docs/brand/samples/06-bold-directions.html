This round keeps what made it credible — the clause numbers, the fee in monospace, the seal — and throws out the politeness: heavier mass, condensed caps at display size, one screaming accent, and the orbit from your existing mark turned into a weapon instead of a decoration.

What the mark actually is

Corrected after a second look at your favicon: it is not a Saturn ring. It is a slipstream — a tapering trail, whip-thin at the tail, fattest where it cuts the planet, with two wake lines chasing it. That taper is the whole personality, and it is what I had drawn wrong.

The rocket — your call

The slipstream is the distinctive part; the rocket is the part a thousand other study-abroad logos also have. Both are drawn and shown below, so you can judge the heritage version against the stripped one rather than take my word for it.

What survives from round one

Fees and certificate IDs in monospace, clause numbers on the guarantee, no gradient, no pill buttons. That's what keeps ₹3,00,000 believable while the rest gets loud.

What changed, and what I need

The mark

Your planet keeps its mass; the orbit now cuts a real notch out of it and carries the accent colour. The rocket is gone. It still reads at 16px, which the current mark does not — and it is one shape, so it works embroidered, embossed or as a single-colour stamp.

The type

Anton for display — condensed, heavy, set enormous. Oswald for the wordmark, which is close to your current lettering. Archivo for body. Fees and certificate IDs stay in IBM Plex Mono.

Still non-negotiable

  • Money and IDs in monospace
  • Clause numbers wherever the guarantee is mentioned
  • Never "guaranteed admission" — it's a remedy, not an admission
  • No gradients, no pills, no stock backpacks

Tell me

  • Heritage or stripped — rocket in, or slipstream alone
  • D1, D2 or D3 — or D3's green with D1's yellow, which also works
  • Bold everywhere, or bold in marketing and quiet in contracts and certificates?
  • Wordmark: "AXELIS OVERSEAS", or "AXELIS" alone with the descriptor small?
