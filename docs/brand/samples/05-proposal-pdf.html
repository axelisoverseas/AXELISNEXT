The written outcome guarantee

Clause 4 — in full

  1. §4.1Axelis agrees a shortlist of at least six schools with the candidate in writing before any application is opened.
  2. §4.2If no offer is received from any shortlisted school, 60% of the programme fee is returned within 30 days of the final decision.
  3. §4.3Not covered: visa refusal on grounds of document fraud or misrepresentation; withdrawal by the candidate; failure to sit a required test on the agreed date.
  4. §4.4Axelis does not guarantee admission. It guarantees the remedy stated in §4.2 on the failure defined in §4.2.
