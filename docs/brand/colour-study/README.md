# Accent colour study

Nine candidates, the same artwork and the same dimensional treatment each time,
so only the colour differs. Contrast is measured against white, which is the
Axelis ground. Regenerate with `python3 scripts/build-3d-mark.py`.

| Candidate | Hex | On white | Grade | Category read |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Graphite | `#2E2E32` | 13.52 | AAA | Unowned. The monochrome play. |
| Forest ink | `#14432A` | 11.25 | AAA | Unowned. Most institutional. |
| **Plum** | `#7A1E56` | **9.78** | AAA | **Unowned, and the most distinctive.** |
| Violet | `#5B21B6` | 8.98 | AAA | Close to AECC's indigo. |
| Crimson (current) | `#A51C30` | 7.48 | AAA | Clear of IIE, which owns brick orange. |
| Bronze | `#8A4B16` | 6.78 | AA | Drifts toward IIE's brick. |
| Emerald | `#046A38` | 6.72 | AA | StudyAbroad's green family. |
| Blue (original) | `#1D4ED8` | 6.70 | AA | Half the category owns blue. |
| Teal | `#0F766E` | 5.47 | AA | StudyAbroad territory. |

## Ruled out on category, not on looks

Teal and Emerald sit in StudyAbroad.com's green-teal family. Violet is close
enough to AECC's indigo to be mistaken for it. Bronze drifts toward IIE's brick
orange. Each is a fine colour that buys Axelis less separation than it should.

## The three worth choosing between

**Plum `#7A1E56`** is the most differentiated: nobody in the category is near it,
and at 9.78 it clears AAA comfortably. It reads premium rather than institutional.

**Forest ink `#14432A`** is the most serious. At 11.25 it is nearly as dark as
body text, so it reads as a considered institution rather than a consumer brand.
It pairs oddly with navy, which is the one thing to check before committing.

**Graphite `#2E2E32`** is the disruptor: in a category of colourful marks, refusing
colour is the loudest move. It also removes the accent-vs-navy problem entirely.

Whichever wins, the accent must clear 4.5:1 on white for text and have a separate
light value for navy grounds, because no dark accent survives on `#16265C`.
